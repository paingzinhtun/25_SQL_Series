# Business Questions

| Business Question | Why It Matters | SQL Concept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| List all marketing channels. | Shows where campaigns are running. | `SELECT`, `ORDER BY` |
| List campaigns with channel information. | Connects campaign setup to channel context. | `JOIN` |
| Show daily ad performance. | Provides daily spend, click, and impression tracking. | `JOIN`, date fields |
| Calculate impressions by campaign. | Measures campaign reach. | `GROUP BY`, `SUM` |
| Calculate clicks by campaign. | Measures traffic generated by campaigns. | `GROUP BY`, `SUM` |
| Calculate spend by campaign. | Shows budget usage. | `SUM`, `GROUP BY` |
| Calculate CTR. | Shows how well impressions turn into clicks. | Formula, `NULLIF` |
| Calculate CPC. | Measures cost efficiency for traffic. | Formula, `NULLIF` |
| Count leads by campaign. | Shows lead generation performance. | `LEFT JOIN`, `COUNT` |
| Count qualified leads. | Measures lead quality. | `FILTER`, aggregation |
| Count customers acquired. | Shows which campaigns create customers. | `LEFT JOIN`, `COUNT` |
| Calculate lead conversion rate. | Shows qualified leads divided by total leads. | Formula, `NULLIF` |
| Calculate customer conversion rate. | Shows customers divided by total leads. | Formula, `NULLIF` |
| Calculate cost per lead. | Connects spend to lead generation. | CTE, formula |
| Calculate CAC. | Connects spend to customer acquisition. | CTE, formula |
| Calculate paid revenue by campaign. | Measures real collected revenue. | Revenue filtering |
| Calculate revenue by channel. | Shows which channels produce business value. | `JOIN`, `GROUP BY` |
| Calculate ROAS. | Shows paid revenue divided by ad spend. | CTE, formula |
| Calculate campaign ROI. | Shows profit-like return after spend. | CTE, formula |
| Find top campaigns by revenue. | Identifies strongest revenue campaigns. | CTE, `RANK()` |
| Find top campaigns by ROI. | Identifies efficient campaigns. | CTE, window function |
| Find high spend low revenue campaigns. | Flags budget waste. | CTE, `WHERE` |
| Find high clicks low conversions. | Finds traffic that is not producing outcomes. | CTE, `WHERE` |
| Find high impressions low CTR. | Finds campaigns with weak creative or targeting. | CTE, CTR formula |
| Find campaigns with no leads. | Shows campaigns that failed to generate prospects. | `LEFT JOIN`, `WHERE IS NULL` |
| Find campaigns with no paid sales. | Shows campaigns without real revenue. | `LEFT JOIN`, revenue filtering |
| Find best channels by ROI. | Helps decide budget allocation by channel. | CTE, `DENSE_RANK()` |
| Calculate monthly marketing spend. | Shows spend trend over time. | `DATE_TRUNC`, `GROUP BY` |
| Calculate monthly paid revenue. | Shows revenue trend over time. | `DATE_TRUNC`, revenue filtering |
| Calculate monthly ROI trend. | Compares spend and revenue by month. | CTE, formula |
| Find lead status distribution. | Shows funnel quality by campaign. | `GROUP BY`, `COUNT` |
| Create a KPI summary. | Produces dashboard-level marketing metrics. | Multiple CTEs, `CASE WHEN` |
| Build a campaign dashboard view. | Combines spend, funnel, revenue, and health in one result. | Multiple CTEs, calculated metrics |
